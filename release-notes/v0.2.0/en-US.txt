New electric guitar app: the full 4-level course (132 lessons), from your first chords to improvising over a backing track, with bends, vibrato, tapping and effects. Includes a songbook of 10 original rock, pop, funk, jazz and gospel songs. In the bass app: all of level 1, staff reading and a 14-song songbook. Both apps work on phones and tablets, in English, Spanish and Portuguese.
